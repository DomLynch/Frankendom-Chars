# Pitborn L8 visual acceptance

L8 is the pilot for the Pitborn rank ladder. Passing the rig checks is necessary but not sufficient.

## Identity

- Must still read immediately as the existing Pitborn/orc-blooded pit brute.
- Existing height, hunch, mass, cleaver and underlying body remain unchanged.
- Armour should look forged for a brutal arena veteran, not a knight, paladin or generic MMO boss.
- Retain at least one visible primitive/bone/trophy motif so the elite metal evolution still connects to lower Pitborn ranks.

## Silhouette

- Full arm and thigh coverage.
- Broad but not oversized shoulders.
- Closed gladiator-style helmet with a compact crest/visor language.
- L8 helmet must leave clear design space for L9's horned executioner structure and L10's infernal gold crown.
- Joint gaps remain readable at elbows, knees, shoulders and groin; no single inflated shell.

## Materials

- Dominant blackened forged steel: dark neutral metal, readable highlights, not flat charcoal.
- Deep ruby accents are small focal elements, not red emissive lights.
- Dark worn leather only where articulation/straps need it.
- Old bone/trophy accent is restrained and physically integrated.
- No bright magical emission.

## Fight-scale readability

At a measured ~110 px fighter height, the viewer should still read:
1. closed helmet,
2. shoulder silhouette,
3. dark-steel tier,
4. ruby elite cue,
5. cleaver/Pitborn identity.

Fine engraving is secondary to those five reads.

## Blocking defects

Do not batch L2–L10 if L8 has any of:
- missing or changed source joints/clips;
- armour detached in Heavy, Guard or Kick;
- face/head/body identity accidentally replaced;
- exposed upper arm or thigh contrary to brief;
- pauldron/helmet clipping that dominates the silhouette;
- generic plate-knight read;
- helmet geometry too similar to the planned L9/L10 structures;
- old renders whose hash does not match the candidate GLB.
