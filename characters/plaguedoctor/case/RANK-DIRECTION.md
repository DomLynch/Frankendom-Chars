# Plague Doctor rank direction

L1 remains byte-identical. All new ranks preserve anatomy, rig, bones, animations and weapon identity.

| Rank | Material | Restrained distinguishing form |
|---|---|---|
| L2 | Brown leather | Sewn shoulder reinforcement, plain leather cuffs |
| L3 | Bone and hide | Small bone shoulder cap, bone wrist toggles |
| L4 | Copper | Copper chest clasp and single layered shoulder plate |
| L5 | Bronze | Paired shoulder plates, reinforced knee guards |
| L6 | Iron | Ribbed chest protection and fitted bracers |
| L7 | Steel | Articulated shoulder and shin plates, stronger gorget |
| L8 | Blackened steel / ruby | Low enclosed beak with short swept temple horns, complete armour |
| L9 | Emerald | Tall angular enclosed beak with swept side fins, complete armour |
| L10 | Full gold | Crowned enclosed beak, feathered shoulders, embossed chest, full gold armoured coat; ultimate boss |

L8–L10 have no exposed face, arms, hands, legs or feet. Each helmet differs in shape. Ruby is non-emissive crimson stone. Surfaces remain worn and grounded; original body size does not increase.

L10 first-fit acceptance precedes new rank generation. Reference art is not fitted-model evidence. Original face remains intact beneath closed armour; disclose original clothing hidden under reconstructed surfaces.
