# Provenance and acceptance

The selected archive catalog was assembled on 2026-09-29 from five separate character tasks. Each selected GLB was compared byte-for-byte by SHA256 with its final local delivery; the archive central directory and every member's CRC were checked. Archive text was scanned for common credential signatures before publication. Only character assets, recipes and evidence are included; no game implementation, account settings or credentials are included.

`catalog.json` records all six original archive hashes and all 45 selected model hashes. Character `case/` folders are text-only snapshots of those archives; read their handoffs as dated evidence. Absolute local paths and historical private HF dataset names in those snapshots are provenance, not usable credentials or a promise of portable execution. Previews are actual exported-model renders. `originalReceiptHasImageHash` distinguishes captures with a pre-existing image hash from captures whose original receipt bound only the model; publication hashes do not retroactively improve that earlier evidence.

Selected inputs:

- Witch: final frontal-face correction, plus source bundle; L10 separate delivery complete. L4/L5 face remnants remain open.
- Knight: final warm L2 and repaired L6/L7, all L2–L10; cuff/shoulder finish remains open.
- Dwarf: targeted closed-helmet/local-coverage delivery, not the discarded whole-suit rebuild; L9 underarm patch and inherited finger/armour defects remain open.
- Nightborn: corrected covered elite revision; earlier lower ranks preserved, previous open-face elite versions superseded.
- Plague Doctor: final animation-restored ladder and L8 coat repair; rough hems/cuffs and subdued material finish remain open.
